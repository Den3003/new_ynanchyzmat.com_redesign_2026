About Us

For more than a decade, Ynanch Hyzmat has delivered engineering, procurement, construction, maintenance, automation, and specialised industrial services to the oil & gas, petrochemical, energy, construction, and infrastructure sectors. Trusted by both national enterprises and international operators, the company combines internationally aligned engineering practices with deep local expertise to execute complex projects safely, efficiently, and to the highest technical standards. Today, with over 1,200 completed projects, more than 300 strategic partners, and licenses covering 21 industrial activities, Ynanch Hyzmat has established itself as one of Turkmenistan's most capable industrial service companies.

As industries evolve and projects become increasingly complex, our purpose remains unchanged: delivering reliable solutions that help clients operate with confidence. This commitment shapes both our mission today and our vision of becoming a lasting pillar of Turkmenistan's industrial development.

History

Ynanch Hyzmat was founded in 2016 with a clear objective: to provide dependable industrial services that simplify complex maintenance and operational challenges for energy operators. Starting with a small team of technical specialists in Ashgabat, the company focused on delivering high-quality industrial maintenance services where precision, safety, and reliability were essential.

Early success in the oil and gas sector created new opportunities. Following its first major shutdown project, Ynanch Hyzmat steadily expanded its capabilities by investing in specialised equipment, workshops, logistics infrastructure, and highly qualified personnel. Strategic partnerships with Production Sharing Agreement (PSA) operators, leading international manufacturers, and original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) enabled the company to broaden its expertise beyond maintenance into engineering, procurement, construction, automation, and integrated industrial solutions.

Over the past decade, this measured and capability-driven growth has transformed Ynanch Hyzmat from a local service provider into one of Turkmenistan's most comprehensive industrial service companies. Today, the company supports state enterprises and international operators with multidisciplinary teams, nationwide operations, and the technical resources required to execute complex industrial projects safely, efficiently, and to consistently high standards.
