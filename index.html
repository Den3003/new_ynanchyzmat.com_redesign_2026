H₂S Dispersion Assessment & Modelling

Advanced Modelling for Safer Oil & Gas Operations
ES Ynanch Hyzmat delivers H₂S Dispersion Assessment and Modelling Services to support informed safety engineering, emergency preparedness, and risk-based decision-making across oil and gas facilities.

Using advanced PHAST 2D and FLACS CFD modelling, we evaluate credible H₂S release scenarios and atmospheric dispersion behaviour to determine potential toxic exposure zones, hazardous areas, and safety-critical impacts.

Our assessments support the engineering and optimisation of:

  • H₂S dispersion and toxic hazard modelling
  • ⁠Consequence assessment for accidental gas releases
  • Gas detector and monitoring system placement
  • ⁠Muster point selection and safe evacuation planning
  • ⁠Emergency response and contingency planning
  • Risk reduction and mitigation measures
  • Advanced 3D CFD analysis for complex release scenarios

By combining engineering expertise, advanced computational modelling, and practical risk assessment, we help operators enhance process safety, protect personnel, strengthen emergency response, and improve the overall resilience of their facilities.
